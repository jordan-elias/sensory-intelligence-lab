Sensory Intelligence Lab

Interactive tools for building sensory awareness: ear training, auditory minigames, emotional regulation, daily listening prompts, and personalized sound-based practices grounded in music neuroscience and therapy.

What's inside

◎
Auditory scene analysis games

Scientifically grounded minigames for attention training, auditory discrimination, and sensory filtering — rare tools at the intersection of music neuroscience and therapy.

◈
Regulation toolkit

Select your current state — overwhelmed, unfocused, overstimulated — and receive a tailored listening sequence, soundscape, and pacing guidance using iso principle and tempo modulation.

◇
Ear training & listening awareness

Pitch and timbre recognition exercises that bridge auditory perception, music neuroscience, and therapeutic listening practice.

◑
Sensory load monitor

Track noise, fatigue, and crowding across your day. See your sensory load score over time and spot patterns — built for people navigating ADHD, autism, burnout, or anxiety.

○
Focus timer with sound regulation

Start a focus session and receive background sound, rhythm, and gentle pacing cues calibrated to support sustained attention.

◻
Daily listening prompts & field recording library

A new listening prompt each day, plus tools to collect, organise, and reuse your own field recordings for soundscape creation and regulation practice.
