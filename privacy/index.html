Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What this platform is

The Sensory Intelligence Lab is a self-directed wellness and learning tool designed to support sensory awareness, emotional regulation, and creative exploration through sound, reflection, and structured exercises.

2. What this platform is not

This platform is not clinical therapy, not a healthcare service, and not a substitute for professional mental health treatment, diagnosis, or emergency care.

It is not monitored in real time and does not provide crisis support.

3. Privacy overview

Your privacy is designed as a core principle of the system. Wherever possible, data is encrypted in a way that prevents access by the platform operator.

4. What data is collected

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Encrypted journal entries and check-ins
  • Basic usage metadata (e.g., session activity for system functionality)

We do not read, access, or interpret your journal content in its decrypted form.

5. Client-side encryption (plain language)

Your journal entries and check-ins are encrypted on your device before they are stored. This means they are turned into unreadable data before they leave your browser.

Even though your data is stored on our servers, it cannot be read without your key.

Each user has a unique encryption key generated at signup. This key is:

  • Encrypted using your password for normal login
  • Also encrypted using a one-time recovery key shown only once

The system never stores your journal data in readable form.

6. Recovery key

Your recovery key is the only way to restore access if you forget your password.

  • It is shown only once during setup
  • It is never stored by the platform
  • If lost, encrypted data cannot be recovered

7. Data sharing

No data is shared by default.

Data is only shared if you explicitly choose to export or share it. In future features, you may optionally re-encrypt specific entries for sharing with a therapist or external party. Nothing is ever shared automatically.

8. Payments

If paid features are introduced, they will be processed through Stripe, a third-party payment provider. We do not store full payment card details.

Full terms: Payment Terms

9. Cancellation

You may cancel your account at any time. Upon cancellation, your encrypted data will be deleted from active systems according to our deletion policy. Some anonymised system logs may remain for technical integrity.

10. GDPR rights (EU users)

If you are located in the EU, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct or update your data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: hello@jordanelias.de

11. Crisis disclaimer

This platform is not monitored and does not provide emergency support. If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline in your country.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy: hello@jordanelias.de

13. FAQ

Can you read my journal entries?

No. They are encrypted client-side before storage and cannot be read without your key. They can only be read if you explicitly choose to share them.

What happens if I lose my recovery key?

Your encrypted data cannot be recovered. This is necessary to maintain end-to-end encryption.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can request full deletion at any time.
