Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you.

Privacy is not a feature added at the end — it is the starting point of how this platform was built. This document explains in plain language what data is collected, how it is stored, how it is protected, and what you can do with it. Technical detail is included deliberately, because vague reassurances are not reassuring.

If something here is unclear, or if you have a question not answered below, write to hello@jordanelias.de. You will get a direct reply.

Who made this

The Sensory Intelligence Lab was built by Jordan Elias, a music therapist based in Berlin. This project is an attempt to build something that could hold continuity between sessions, support self-directed practice, and make music therapy tools accessible to people who cannot easily access in-person care.

Jordan Elias is the data controller for this platform. All questions, requests, and concerns go directly to one person, not a support team.

Your content and your privacy

The journal, check-ins, worksheets, saved places and recordings are personal tools. The platform is built around two rules: nothing is recorded unless you choose to save it, and nothing you save is shared with anyone unless you choose to share it. Sharing is never switched on by default.

The three options for anything you write Save — your entry is encrypted in your browser before it is sent to the server. The server stores scrambled data only. Only you can unlock it, with your vault passphrase or recovery key.

Download as a file — your entry is written directly to a file on your own device. Nothing is sent to any server. The file belongs entirely to you and is not encrypted.

Delete / Destroy — the text is erased from the page and from memory. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Writing, downloading and deleting are private in every case. No drafts are auto-saved to the server, and no keystroke or word-count data is sent while you write. The only moment saved content leaves your browser is when you press Save, and by then it is already encrypted.

Working with a therapist on the platform

If you are on a session plan you can connect to a therapist. Until you do, no therapist can see you at all, and free and Lab-only accounts are never visible to therapists. When you connect, you are shown exactly what the therapist will see before you agree: your name, your email, your subscription and intake status, and the times of the sessions you book with them. Nothing else is visible until you share it.

  • Check-ins are shared only if you switch sharing on for that therapist, and you choose whether notes are included.
  • Worksheets are shared one document at a time. There is no switch that shares them all.
  • Messages between you and your therapist can be read only by the two of you.
  • Journal entries, recordings and saved places are not shareable through the platform.

Shared items are re-encrypted so that only you and that therapist can open them. You can stop sharing, and either of you can end the connection, at any time. When a connection ends, you and the therapist each choose whether to keep or delete your own copy. You can also still download any file and send it by email if you prefer.

How encryption works

Saved content is encrypted in your browser before it is sent to any server. What the database holds is scrambled data that cannot be read without your key. The encryption key is protected by a passphrase that only you know.

Your vault

The first time you save something private you create a vault passphrase. Your browser turns it into a key using Argon2id (a deliberately slow function that makes guessing hard), and that key protects the random content key that encrypts your data. You also receive a recovery key, a second way to open the same vault if you forget the passphrase. The server stores only the protected bundle. It never receives your passphrase, your recovery key, or the unprotected content key.

Technical specification

Content encryption: AES-256-GCM with a fresh random 96-bit IV for every save, in your browser using the Web Crypto API

Passphrase protection: Argon2id (64 MiB, 3 passes) derives the key that wraps your content key

Recovery key: 160 random bits, a second wrapper for the same content key

Sharing keys: each account has an ECDH P-256 key pair for receiving shared items and an ECDSA P-256 key pair for signing messages. The private halves are encrypted with your content key. Shared items and messages are sealed separately to each person allowed to read them.

Binding: each encrypted item is bound to your account and its purpose, so it cannot be moved into another place unnoticed

What this means in practice

What this protects against Someone who obtains a copy of the database, the platform operator included, sees only scrambled data for everything marked encrypted below. There is no server-side copy of your key that the operator could use to read it.
Honest limitations If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery key, your encrypted content cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. Resetting your account password does not unlock it.

Not everything is encrypted. Your email, name, subscription, timestamps, booking times and the size of what you store are visible to the platform. Game scores and instrument or mixer settings are stored unencrypted. The table below lists each item.

This is a website. Your browser downloads the platform's code from our server each time. Encryption in the browser protects you against database access, but not against a malicious or compromised version of the site itself. We keep the number of outside code sources small and are working to remove the remaining ones.

Identity separation

Your content is attached to a random account ID, not to your name or email, which are held in separate tables. Anyone looking only at content tables would see scrambled data under anonymous IDs. This is a precaution, not a guarantee: the platform operator can link IDs to people.

Supabase and the database

This platform uses Supabase as its database and authentication provider — an open-source alternative to Firebase, built on PostgreSQL and hosted on AWS. The database is in the EU West (Frankfurt) region, keeping all stored data within the European Union.

Row Level Security (RLS)

Every table has Row Level Security enabled. RLS is a PostgreSQL feature that enforces access control at the database level — not just in application code. Even if there were a bug in the application, the database itself would refuse to return data the requesting user is not authorised to see.

RLS policy structure

Your own data: check-ins, worksheets, journal entries, recordings, saved places and your profile can be read, changed and deleted only by the account that owns them.

Therapists: a therapist can see an account only through the connection you created, and only the few fields listed above. Free and Lab-only accounts do not appear to therapists at all.

Shared items and messages: readable only by the two people they were sealed to, and even then only as scrambled data that their own browser opens.

Key material: your protected vault bundle can be read only by you.

The service role key

Supabase provides two API keys: an anon key (used in the browser, subject to RLS) and a service role key (bypasses RLS, for server use only). The service role key is stored only in Netlify environment variables and is never exposed to the browser. The vault function that handles encryption keys uses the service role key only after verifying the user's session token.

Supabase's own privacy commitments

Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and GDPR-ready, with encryption at rest and in transit. Read their privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy. Jordan Elias is the data controller — Supabase acts as a data processor.

Data we collect

We collect only what is needed for the platform to work. There is no advertising, no third-party tracking and no analytics that profile your behaviour.

Account data

Your email address, your chosen display name, and your password (hashed by Supabase; we never see it).

Content you choose to save

Journal entries, check-ins, saved places, worksheets, recordings and messages, stored encrypted as described above. Downloaded or deleted content is never recorded.

Session and technical data

Supabase logs sign-ins and token refreshes for security, and our hosting provider processes your IP address as part of delivering the site. We run no analytics or tracking scripts, no marketing trackers, no pixels and no fingerprinting.

Booking data

When you book a session, the booking is made on Cal.com or, if your therapist uses their own calendar, on their own booking page. That service receives your name, email and chosen time and has its own privacy policy. The platform records only when the session is and which therapist it is with, so that sessions can be counted and shown to you and to that therapist.

Intake

The platform stores only an intake status (for example, not started or complete). It is visible to a therapist you connect with. The platform does not store the contents of any intake forms.

Cookies The platform uses session storage set by Supabase to keep you signed in, plus settings stored in your browser (for example, which device you trust for your vault). There are no advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

Data sharing

Your rights under GDPR

As a resident of the EU, UK, Switzerland, or another jurisdiction with equivalent data protection law, you have the following rights. To exercise any of them, write to hello@jordanelias.de. We will respond within 30 days.

Right of access

Your saved content is visible to you inside the platform, and “Download my data” on the Account page gives you a copy. For anything else we hold about you, contact us. Because your content is encrypted, only you can open it.

Right to rectification

You can update your name and email from the Account page. For anything else, contact us.

Right to erasure

You can delete individual items from within the platform, and delete your whole account yourself from the Account page. See below.

Right to data portability

On the Account page, “Download my data” saves everything stored for you (journal, check-ins, saved places, worksheets, messages, bookings and scores) to one file on your own device. Audio recordings are listed in it, and can be downloaded one by one.

Right to object / restrict processing

Contact us to discuss any concerns about how your data is being processed.

Lawful basis for processing

We process your data under GDPR Article 6 on the bases of performance of a contract (providing the service), legitimate interests (security logging, fraud prevention), and consent (for any processing not covered by the above, including everything you choose to share with a therapist). Health-related data under Article 9 is processed with your explicit consent.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself from the Account page. It takes effect straight away and removes your login, your profile, all of your saved content, your recordings and files, your keys, your messages and shared items (including the copies held by a therapist), your booking records and your therapist connections. It also cancels your subscription immediately.

Some records are kept by others and are outside the platform: Stripe keeps the payment records it is legally required to keep, and Supabase's backups are cleared on their own schedule. Your encrypted content in any such backup cannot be read without your passphrase or recovery key.

Important Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Download anything you want to keep first. If you are connected to a therapist, that therapist's copies of what you shared are deleted too, and your message history with them is deleted for both of you. You can also request deletion by writing to hello@jordanelias.de.
