Experimental Compositions

Learn compositional techniques from experimental music pioneers. Each tool combines interactive sound design with education about a key work and artistic philosophy.

◈
Phase Machine
Explore tape phasing through two synchronized loops that gradually drift apart, creating emergent rhythmic patterns. Based on Steve Reich's "It's Gonna Rain."
Steve Reich
→
◒
Rooms
Iteratively process audio through acoustic room filters to watch how resonances emerge and evolve. Inspired by Alvin Lucier's "I Am Sitting in a Room."
Alvin Lucier
→
◐
Waves
Compose slowly evolving drones using detuned oscillators and imperceptible drift. Inspired by Éliane Radigue's hypnotic, meditative synthesizer pieces.
Éliane Radigue
→
▤
Neural Synthesis
Explore a neural network of sound-generating and signal-processing nodes connected by weighted edges. Sound emerges from the interaction of the system with itself.
David Tudor
→
▣
Oramics
Draw directly onto a moving film strip. Your shapes become sound — pitch, amplitude, timbre, modulation.
Daphne Oram
→

The sound of natural language processing.
