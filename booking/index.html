How sessions work

Sessions are held online via video call. You don't need any prior music experience or equipment beyond headphones.

Sessions are not music lessons. They draw on music therapy, somatic approaches, and neuroscience to support regulation, sensory processing, and nervous system care.

After your request is submitted, Jordan will reply with available times and a short intake form if this is your first session.
