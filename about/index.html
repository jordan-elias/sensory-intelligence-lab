Sensory Intelligence Lab

What this is

Sensory Intelligence Lab is a collection of tools, references, and curated resources exploring perception, attention, sound, cognition, and lived sensory experience.

It is designed as an open, evolving space for learning and exploration rather than a fixed product.

Purpose

The goal is to support deeper understanding of how humans process sound, music, identity, and environment — and to provide accessible pathways into research, art, and practice.

It brings together scientific ideas, creative exploration, and open educational resources in one place.

Who I am

I am a music therapist and cognitive neuroscientist interested in the intersection of sound, mental health, and perception.

My work focuses on how auditory experience shapes emotion, attention, and cognition, and how structured sound-based approaches can support wellbeing and self-understanding.

How to use this space

Browse freely, follow connections between sections, and use it as a starting point for deeper exploration rather than a complete or authoritative system.
