Common questions

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time? +
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time via the billing portal. Changes to a lower tier take effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep access until then. Upgrades take effect immediately.
What happens to unused video sessions? +
Unused sessions carry over for one month. If you miss two months in a row, the sessions expire. This is to keep the scheduling manageable — video sessions are a finite resource on Jordan's end.
How does payment work? +
Payment is handled entirely by Stripe — a secure, industry-standard payment processor. Your card details are never stored on this site. You can update your payment method or view invoices at any time through the billing portal.
What are the video sessions for? +
The sessions are one-to-one video calls with Jordan, the music therapist behind these tools. They can be used for guided listening work, discussion of what you've been exploring in the Lab, or support around goals.
Is the free tier really free? +
Yes — no credit card required, no trial period. The Regulation Engine, Breath Regulation, Ear Training, and Resource Library are free indefinitely.
What is the difference between monthly and yearly billing? +
Yearly billing gives you the equivalent of 10 months for the price of 12 — a saving of roughly 17%. The features are identical; you just pay once per year rather than once per month. You can switch between billing intervals when you change your plan.
