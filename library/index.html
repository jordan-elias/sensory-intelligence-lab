Books & Reading (Public Domain & Free Texts)

Aesthetic experience, perception, consciousness

  • William James – The Principles of Psychology (1890)
    Focus: Attention, habit formation, consciousness, perception, early cognitive psychology foundations.
  • Immanuel Kant – Critique of Judgment
    Focus: Aesthetic judgment, sensory experience, beauty without concept, perception and meaning.
  • John Dewey – Art as Experience
    Focus: Embodied aesthetics, perception as lived experience, meaning-making through interaction.
    Note: not public domain, but widely available via educational and archival scans.
  • Wassily Kandinsky - Point and Line to Plane
    Focus: Visual perception, abstraction, spatial relationships, and the emotional and structural meaning of basic geometric forms in art and design.
  • Albert Bregman - Auditory scene analysis : the perceptual organization of sound
    Focus: psychoacoustics, speech perception, music theory and composition, and computer modeling

Psychoacoustics & sensory science

  • Hermann von Helmholtz – On the Sensations of Tone
    Focus: Pitch perception, resonance, harmonic structure, early auditory neuroscience.
  • Auditory Neuroscience Resources
  • Gustav Fechner – Elements of Psychophysics
    Focus: Sensory thresholds, perception scaling, quantitative approaches to sensation.

Emotion, body, and perception

  • Charles Darwin – The Expression of the Emotions in Man and Animals (1872)
    Focus: Emotional expression, bodily signaling, evolutionary foundations of affect.
  • Henri Bergson – Matter and Memory
    Focus: Time, memory, perception, continuity of experience, embodied cognition.

Media, attention, and perception

  • Hugo Münsterberg – The Photoplay: A Psychological Study
    Focus: Attention, visual perception, narrative cognition, early media psychology.
