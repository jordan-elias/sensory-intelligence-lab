Books — open access

Perception & aesthetics

The Principles of Psychology William James, 1890 Attention, habit formation, consciousness, and early cognitive psychology. Free · Gutenberg Critique of Judgment Immanuel Kant Aesthetic judgment, sensory experience, and beauty without concept. Free · Gutenberg Art as Experience John Dewey Embodied aesthetics and meaning-making through interaction. Archive.org scan Point and Line to Plane Wassily Kandinsky Visual perception, abstraction, and the emotional meaning of geometric forms. Archive.org scan

Psychoacoustics & sensory science

On the Sensations of Tone Hermann von Helmholtz Pitch perception, resonance, harmonic structure, early auditory neuroscience. Archive.org scan Auditory Scene Analysis Albert Bregman The perceptual organisation of sound. Foundational psychoacoustics text. Archive.org scan Elements of Psychophysics Gustav Fechner Sensory thresholds, perception scaling, and quantitative approaches to sensation. Archive.org scan Auditory Neuroscience Resources auditoryneuroscience.com Freely available companion site to the textbook. Demos, data, and chapter materials. Free · Web

Emotion, body & memory

The Expression of the Emotions in Man and Animals Charles Darwin, 1872 Emotional expression, bodily signalling, and evolutionary foundations of affect. Free · Gutenberg Matter and Memory Henri Bergson Time, memory, perception, continuity of experience, embodied cognition. Archive.org scan
