Library

A curated collection of freely accessible resources for exploring sound, listening, creativity, regulation, and wellbeing. Updated regularly.

Free music & audio tools

Audacity — Free, open-source audio recording and editing software. Cross-platform.

SuperCollider — Audio synthesis and algorithmic composition environment. Includes beginner tutorials.

LMMS — Free, open-source digital audio workstation with beat editor and synthesisers.

Bespoke Synth — Modular music creation environment. Highly visual and experimental.

Hundred Rabbits — Independent studio developing minimalist creative tools.

OBS Studio — Open-source recording and streaming software.
