How the soundscapes work

Each soundscape is generated in real time rather than played from a recording. A slow drone, a melodic voice and a soft rhythm are shaped by the two states you choose: each state has its own scale, register, speed, tone and amount of echo, and the sound moves gradually from the character of your current state to the character of your target. Because the sounds are generated, no two sessions are exactly the same, even when you select the same options.

The journey follows the iso principle from music therapy. Instead of switching to a new sound at once, the music begins by matching where you are and then moves step by step toward where you want to be. The structure draws on ideas from music cognition and deep listening. It is a listening experience, not a treatment, and it makes no claims about what it does to your body.
