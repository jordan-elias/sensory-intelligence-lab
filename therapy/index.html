Therapy

One-to-one video sessions with a music therapist, connected to the work you do in the app, so the hour goes further.

How the app and therapy fit together

1. Work on your own, any dayCheck in, listen, practise regulation, try the tools. All of it is private to you. Nobody sees any of it by default.
2. Choose what to bringIf you want your therapist to see your work, you share it. Check-ins can be shared with a switch you control. Worksheets are shared one at a time. You can stop sharing whenever you like.
3. Meet, with contextYour therapist starts from what you have been noticing, rather than from scratch. Less catching up, more depth.
4. Carry it back into your daysKeep checking in between sessions. Your own patterns stay on your device, and you decide what is worth raising.

What your therapist can see

When you connect, your therapist sees your name, your email, whether you have a Lab plan, your intake status, and the times of the sessions you book with them. Nothing else, unless you share it. Messages between the two of you can be read only by you and them.

You can end the connection at any time and choose what happens to what was shared. Read the full details.

Getting started

  • Introductory session: complimentary, once, before your first regular session. It is a chance to meet and see if it feels right.
  • You can change therapist whenever you need to.
  • You pay when you book, in the booking step. There is no therapy subscription.
