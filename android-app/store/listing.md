## Full description (4000 characters)
Track your progress without being tracked.

Sensory Intelligence Lab is a set of private tools for regulating, understanding and expressing what you feel, using sound, writing and music. It was built by a music therapist who wanted progress tracking that does not depend on handing your inner life to a company.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Check-ins, journal entries, worksheets, recordings and messages are encrypted on your device with a key only you hold.
- The trends and insights you see (how your mood moves over days, weeks and across a day) are calculated on your device.
- No ads. No analytics. No tracking scripts.
- Nothing reaches a therapist unless you choose to send it, and you can take it back.

WHAT IS INSIDE
- Check-ins: log mood, energy and sensory load in a few seconds, then see your own patterns.
- Regulation and breathing: sound-based tools for calming and focusing.
- Journal and worksheets: private by default.
- Experiments in Creativity: a minicourse of 30+ creative prompts and guided activities for getting curious about how you make things and breaking out of creative blocks, covering music, sound and writing. It also works on paper.
- Sequencer, soundscape machine, ear training and experimental compositions, including a small neural network that never stops learning.
- Sessions: book with a music therapist, and send them only what you choose to share.

SUBSCRIPTION
The Lab subscription gives access to every tool. Therapy sessions are booked separately.

This app is not a substitute for medical or psychological care. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number.
